The Key Setting

The Canon R6 Mark II has a portrait-specific feature most operators never find — the Eye Detection AF sensitivity control. Under the AF menu find the AF3 tab and locate Tracking sensitivity. Set this to Locked on rather than Responsive for portrait sessions where your subject is relatively stationary. This tells the camera to maintain lock on the subject's eye even when something passes between you and them — a common occurrence in busy indoor environments. Responsive mode is correct for sports. Locked on is correct for portraits. Most operators leave it on the default middle setting and wonder why the camera occasionally loses the eye when someone walks through the background.
