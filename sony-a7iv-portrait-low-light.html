Sony A7IV Portrait Low Light Settings

Exact settings for keeping your images clean and tack-sharp when the light dies down.
Recommended ModeManual (M) Shutter Speed1/160s (Hold firm) Aperturef/1.4–f/1.8 ISOAuto, cap at 6400 Focus ModeAF-C / Human Eye Priority Drive ModeLow Continuous White Balance4500K (Kelvin Custom)

The Key Setting

Dim urban environments require you to trust the A7IV's BSI sensor processing pipeline. While 33 megapixels can amplify the visual size of chroma noise grain compared to older 24MP bodies, your weapon is configuration discipline. Swap from standard Auto White Balance over to a manual Kelvin rating near 4500K to prevent streetlights from shifting your skin tones into sickly orange hues. Set your ISO ceiling to 6400. The second gain stage of this dual-native sensor clears up significantly at higher ranges, ensuring that noise is fine-grained and organic rather than destructive to face textures.
